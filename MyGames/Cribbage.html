📖 Cribbage Rules & Scoring Guide

Goal: Be the first player to peg 121 points (or 61 in short match).

1. Deal & The Crib: Each player is dealt 6 cards. Both choose 2 cards to discard into the Crib (belonging to the dealer). Hands now have 4 cards each.

2. The Starter Cut: The non-dealer cuts the deck. If the starter card is a Jack, the dealer immediately pegs 2 points ("Two for his heels!").

3. The Play (Pegging to 31): Players alternate playing cards face up without exceeding a running count of 31. Points awarded during play:

  • 15 for 2: Exactly 15 count scores 2 pts.
  • 31 for 2: Exactly 31 count scores 2 pts.
  • Pairs: 2 of same rank consecutive = 2 pts. 3 of a kind = 6 pts. 4 of a kind = 12 pts.
  • Runs: 3 or more consecutive cards in any order = 1 pt per card (e.g. 7-8-6 = 3 pts).
  • Go / Last Card: If you cannot play under 31, say "Go". Player who plays the last legal card pegs 1 pt (unless 31 was scored). Then count resets to 0.

4. The Show (Counting): Non-dealer counts first, then dealer counts hand, then dealer counts the crib. All 4 cards plus the starter cut are evaluated:

  • 15s: 2 pts for every combination of cards totaling 15.
  • Pairs: 2 pts per pair (Jack & Queen do NOT pair).
  • Runs: 1 pt per card in sequence (duplicates multiply, e.g. double run of 3 = 8 pts).
  • Flush: 4 cards in hand same suit = 4 pts (+1 if starter matches). In the crib, all 5 cards MUST match for 5 pts (4-card flush in crib scores 0!).
  • His Nobs: Jack in hand/crib matching the starter card's suit = 1 pt.

🎯 Grid Draft Mode: Each player receives 3 private cards, then drafts 3 more from a face-up 3×3 grid in snake order (non-dealer first in rounds 1 & 3; dealer first in round 2). Every drafted card is immediately replaced from the deck. Once both players hold six cards, discards, the cut, pegging, and the Show proceed exactly as in Classic Cribbage.

Skunking: Winning before your opponent reaches 90 points (in a 121 game) is a Skunk! Reaching 121 before they reach 60 is a Double Skunk! 🦨
