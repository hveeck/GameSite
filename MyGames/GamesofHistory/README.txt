GAMES OF HISTORY — OFFLINE EDITION
==================================

HOW TO START
1. Unzip Games_of_History.zip into a normal folder.
2. Double-click index.html to open the game collection in your browser.
3. Choose Royal Game of Ur, Hnefatafl, or Rithmomachia.
4. Choose a bot difficulty, choose your side, and start playing.

HOW TO LEARN
Open rules.html for a short, beginner-friendly guide to all three games.
Every game also has a Rules & History button with its own complete rules.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Royal Game of Ur: Finkel-style seven-piece race with four binary dice,
  rosettes, captures, extra turns, and exact bearing-off.
- Hnefatafl: 11-by-11 Copenhagen-style Viking king-escape game.
- Rithmomachia: 16-by-8 medieval mathematical strategy game with four bots.

Everything needed to play is inside this folder. No account or internet
connection is required for game logic. If offline, your browser may substitute
its built-in fonts for the optional web fonts.

The historical rule traditions have variants. The in-game guides explain
which rules are implemented and any simplified edge cases.
