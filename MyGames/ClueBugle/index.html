How to play

Two teams compete to uncover their secret agents hidden among 25 words. Each team has one Spymaster, who sees which words belong to each team, and Guessers, who see only the words.

  1. The starting team has 9 agents; the other has 8. There are 7 bystanders and one assassin.
  2. On your turn, your Spymaster gives one word and one number. The word connects to your team's unrevealed agents; the number says how many.
  3. Guessers select one word at a time. A correct guess lets them continue, up to the clue's number plus one total guesses. They can stop early.
  4. A neutral word or an opposing team's word ends the turn. An opponent's word helps the opponent. The assassin loses the game immediately.
  5. The first team to reveal all its agents wins. The Spymaster may not give hints beyond the clue and number. Clues cannot be words visible on the board (including their simple forms).

In Solo mode there are exactly three computer players: your teammate, the opposing Spymaster, and the opposing Guesser. Choose whether you want to be your team's Spymaster or Guesser.
