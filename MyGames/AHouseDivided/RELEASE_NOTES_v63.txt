A House Divided v63 — Interaction Sounds

Based on v62, including American peacetime music, difficulty balancing, and nearest-front automatic merging.

NEW
- Subtle offline sound effects for recruitment (rally), spending (coin clink), military orders,
  diplomacy, policy choices, peace treaties, and ordinary navigation.
- Short, low-volume effects are triggered by human clicks, never by AI actions or automatic time advancement.
- Sound concurrency and repeat throttles prevent large overlapping noise bursts.
- The dramatic cue no longer plays when an ordinary division is lost, for random neutral wars,
  for routine peace treaties, or when unrelated countries capitulate. It is reserved for a severe
  danger event affecting the player country, such as the player's capitulation.
- No added music controls.

Existing background music remains locally bundled for offline play.
