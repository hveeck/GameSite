A HOUSE DIVIDED v2.0.6 — Independence Peace & Manpower

RECRUITMENT
- Starting recruitment is 5% of eligible home and integrated population.
- Available policies: Limited 3%, Standard 5%, Expanded 10%, Mobilized 15%.
- Lower recruitment policies modestly benefit stability and taxes; expansion/mobilization have continuing costs.
- AI decision thresholds updated to use new percentage choices.
- No monthly refill or free troops from changing policy. Mercenaries still use no manpower.

SECESSION / PEACE
- Rebel countries created by the regional uprising mechanic remember their former parent.
- Rebels are much more willing to accept white peace with their former parent, and their former parent gains a smaller willingness bonus.
- Rebel leaders offer status quo peace more readily and can begin offering after 90 days instead of 180 days.
- White peace confirms the borders at the outbreak of the rebellion: breakaway counties retain independence, wartime occupations revert. No concessions or annexations.
- Normal wars and peace calculations are unchanged.
