ABOUT THE MODEL

A map of worlds that never were.

Each generation divides the United States into fictional successor countries using over 3,000 county-scale territories. Borders can cross existing state lines. Geographic county polygons are real, while political divisions are invented. State population totals approximate 2024 Census estimates, distributed to counties with a simplified area-based weighting. Energy, agriculture, industry, ports and cultural-regional affinity are illustrative indexes.

Each simulated year, countries manage stability and capacity. Neighbors may sign defense treaties, dispute territory or fight. Wars compare population, economic output, resources, cohesion, alliances and chance. Counties change hands in fighting; prolonged conflicts create estimated displacement and instability. Rebellions may create new countries.

Important: Country names, political behavior, war results, displacement and political scenarios are fictional game mechanics, not statistical forecasts. County population allocation is not a census county estimate. The game omits nuclear weapons, granular military logistics and many humanitarian and geopolitical factors.

Tip: zoom and drag the map, switch layers, select counties, and advance year by year—or run automatically. Restarting a seed with the same settings reproduces the opening partition.
