A HOUSE DIVIDED v65 — MERCENARY / MANPOWER / INTEGRATION UI REPAIR

Why mercenaries were hidden in v64:
The compact Military interface in minimal.js replaced the earlier national recruiting
interface from multi-front.js. The former had only Militia and Veterans in its
recruitment dropdown, despite mercenaries existing in the core game engine.
Its older recruitment handler also omitted manpower spending and eligibility checks.

Fixes:
- Military's actual compact recruitment menu now shows Mercenaries (295 treasury
  on Regular), with their 18 strength and no-population tradeoff.
- Recruiting from the compact menu now properly spends recruitable population
  (for nonmercenaries) and only uses home or fully integrated territory.
- Government now displays Recruitable population immediately next to Population.
- Government and National Cabinet now show explicit Regional Integration controls.
  Local integration: pay 35 for +25 percentage points to a selected county.
  National integration: pay 90 for +8 points across all conquered counties.
  Both actions share a 90-day cooldown.
- Existing passive integration and Regional Integration initiative continue to work.
- Visible version label and JavaScript/CSS cache identifiers updated to v65.

Note: A v65 label alone still cannot verify all game assets were uploaded; check
that minimal.js and v65-ui.js exist alongside index.html in the published folder.
