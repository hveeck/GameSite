A HOUSE DIVIDED — BACKGROUND MUSIC, v61
======================================
Soundtrack is bundled locally and plays offline. There are no in-game music controls.
Audio begins after the user deliberately enters or starts the simulation, as required by browser autoplay rules.

These are NEW, SYNTHESIZED ARRANGEMENTS of PUBLIC-DOMAIN MELODIES, not historical or professional orchestra recordings. The melodies and accompaniment are simplified adaptations, deliberately looped and not purported to be full original scores.

PEACETIME ROTATION
- Shenandoah — traditional American folk melody, 19th century; newly synthesized arrangement.
- America the Beautiful — public-domain tune Materna, Samuel A. Ward (1882); newly synthesized adaptation.
- Yankee Doodle — traditional American song, public-domain tune.

WARTIME ROTATION
- When Johnny Comes Marching Home — Patrick Gilmore (1863). Public-domain composition; simplified arrangement.
- Battle Hymn of the Republic — tune based on traditional John Brown's Body (19th century); simplified arrangement.
- Eine kleine Nachtmusik — adapted motif from K. 525, Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart (1787). Public-domain composition.

SHORT ORIGINAL EVENT CUES
- War declaration — original instrumental stinger.
- Peace treaty / capitulation — original instrumental stinger.
