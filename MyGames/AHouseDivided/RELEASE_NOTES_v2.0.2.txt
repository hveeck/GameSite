A HOUSE DIVIDED v2.0.2 — MILITARY BALANCE & GOVERNMENT UI

- Military Strength tier labels wrap safely within their Government metric tile.
- War Taxes, War Production, and National Propaganda are compact, single-row
  controls with styled switches, keyboard focus, and disabled state.
- AI countries gradually recruit affordable home-territory divisions in peace
  and war, aiming for a balanced military posture instead of sitting
  underdefended indefinitely. This complements existing wartime response AI.
- Recurring affordability and recruitable manpower checks prevent infinite
  buildup; Easy, Medium, and Hard have different force limits.
- All rules and soundtrack from v2.0.1 are retained.
