Model assumptions: The FY2025 federal receipts total is the anchored baseline. Individual tax-category allocations, assumed tax bases, and GDP multipliers are illustrative. Proposed taxes start at zero. Raising a rate is modeled as additional receipts on a fixed tax base; the game does not forecast tax avoidance, inflation, work incentives, interest rates or real-world incidence. The remittance tax began in 2026 and is not part of FY2025 baseline receipts. The United States has no broad federal sales tax today; the sales/VAT controls are hypothetical. State and local taxes are outside the federal budget.

Official FY2025 federal baseline: U.S. Treasury outlays and receipts. GDP reference: OMB/FRED FY2025 nominal GDP. 2026 remittance tax. The simulated economic impacts use game assumptions informed in broad direction by CBO fiscal policy analysis, not CBO estimates for these exact plans. Program splits and happiness scores are illustrative. No modeled second-round feedback from GDP to tax collections. Your plan stays in your browser.
How to play

Your goal is to eliminate the $1,775.4 billion FY2025 federal deficit—or create a surplus to pay down debt. You can cut spending, increase taxes, or combine both.

  1. Budget overview: See spending, receipts and the remaining deficit.
  2. Spending: Expand categories and adjust individual program funding using sliders.
  3. Your cuts: See what you've reduced and restore any change.
  4. Taxation: Adjust existing tax systems or introduce hypothetical federal taxes.
  5. Outcomes: Watch debt payoff, happiness and modeled GDP respond.
  6. Share: Copy a link to recreate your entire plan.

Treasury totals are real FY2025 figures; detailed allocations and economic effects are simplified game assumptions.
